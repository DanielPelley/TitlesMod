Rarity & scarcity

Rarity sets presentation and the power band. Scarcity sets how many players may hold the title at once. Unique is the one word that appears in both columns and means something different in each — read scarcity for the holder count, rarity for the presentation.

  • Titles are never revoked. Once earned a title is yours permanently — it survives death, inactivity and dimension change. The sole exception is the Contested type, currently The Unrivaled alone, which passes to whoever beats the holder's score.
  • Secret is not a rarity. It is a yes/no flag orthogonal to rarity — a secret title has normal rarity and normal power, the player just doesn't know it exists until it fires. Each one carries a hint string the Titles screen reveals once partial progress is detected.
  • Starter roll weight is the relative chance of that rarity in the free title granted on first join. Mythic and Unique are never rolled — they are only ever earned.

Effect catalog

Every effect code used on the cards above, with its 1.20.1 implementation hook. Rows flagged in red have no vanilla attribute on 1.20.1 and need an event hook instead — the standing cost of the retarget.

  • On 1.20.1 an attribute modifier is keyed by UUID, not ResourceLocation. Store a ResourceLocation-shaped id in the title JSON and derive the UUID with UUID.nameUUIDFromBytes so content files survive the NeoForge port untouched.
  • Always remove modifiers on unequip and on logout-save. Orphaned max_health modifiers are the classic way this kind of mod corrupts a save.
  • 12 of the 23 ATTR_ effects have no vanilla attribute on 1.20.1 — all were added in 1.20.5 / 1.21. ATTR_SNEAKING_SPEED and ATTR_EXPLOSION_KB_RESIST are flagged to cut for v1.
