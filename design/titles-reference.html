The rarity ladder

Rarity sets three things at once: the point budget a title may spend, how likely it is to be your free first-join roll, and how loudly the client announces it. Hex values are the canon Minecraft chat-format colors from 03-Rarity-and-Scarcity.csv; the swatches below show those true values, while the table above shifts them per theme so they stay legible.

Scarcity types

Titles are never revoked. Once earned a title is yours permanently — it survives death, inactivity and dimension change. Contested is the sole exception.

Balance rules

10 equip slotshard

Enough room for a real build identity.

40-point budget on equipped titlesserver configurable

Slots alone are not a limiter — 10 Legendary titles would be ~95 points. The budget is what actually holds the mod together.

Scarce titles are budget-exemptUnique · Limited · Contested · Founding

They stack on top of the 10 rather than competing for a slot.

Scarce ceiling: 5 points of raw stats eachthe rule that makes exemption safe

A scarce title earns its rarity through a signature power and public prestige, not big numbers.

Signature power, not DPSone memorable non-stat ability

Fall immunity, water breathing, sculk invisibility, a revive. Impressive to have and to watch.

Per-attribute hard capssecond safety net
